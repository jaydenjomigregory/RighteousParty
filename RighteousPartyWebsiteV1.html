Our Detailed Policies

Real action over weak promises. Here is our exact blueprint for Peachgrove:

1. Next-Level Grounds & Wildlife

We are dedicated to taking genuine pride in our school environment. Campus aesthetics directly impact student well-being and focus.

  • Plant vibrant floral gardens around key student common areas.
  • Active maintenance and protection of local campus wildlife.
  • Ensure clean, green, and elite grounds standard across Peachgrove.

2. Focused Desk Layouts

Every student deserves an environment free from constant classroom disruptions so they can unlock their full academic potential.

  • Push for separated individual desk setups in classrooms.
  • Create dedicated zones for quiet concentration and lock-in work.
  • Eliminate desk crowding to reduce unwanted distractions.

3. Pro-Tier Sports Gear

No student should have to play with damaged, flat, or outdated sports equipment during PE or lunchtime breaks.

  • Replace all worn-out PE equipment with high-quality gear.
  • Expand lunchtime sports gear availability across all year levels.
  • Upgrade competitive equipment for school sports teams.

4. Yearly School Trips

Learning shouldn't be confined to a desk. We believe in practical, memorable experience-based outdoor education.

  • Schedule an awesome, hands-on school trip every single term.
  • Ensure trip activities reward student hard work throughout the year.
  • Guarantee equal access for all students across classes.

5. Anti-Bullying & Inclusion

We stand firmly against "bandwagon hating" and social exclusion, replacing peer pressure with peace and harmony.

  • Zero-tolerance for targeting students based on personal preferences.
  • Ensure every student feels heard by teachers and principal leadership.
  • Create an inclusive culture where no one gets sidelined or judged.

6. Transparent Governance

Rooted in our core mottos, we pledge honest representation and accountability to the entire student body.

  • Honor every policy platform commitment made during the election.
  • Maintain open communication between leadership and Room 12.
  • Live out our primary mandate: "First Learn To Serve."
